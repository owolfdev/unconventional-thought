```
Did Bon Scott write the lyrics on Back in Black?

Five months. That's all it took after Bon Scott's death for AC/DC to release the second best-selling album in history — 50 million copies. Brian Johnson walked in, auditioned, and supposedly wrote lyrics that rival anything in rock history. But does the evidence actually support that?

In this video, we examine the lyrical fingerprint that Bon Scott left across his entire career — the narrator, the swagger, the wit, the double entendres — and ask a simple question: where does that fingerprint show up on Back in Black, and where did it go in every AC/DC album after?

We look at:
• Bon Scott's unmistakable writing style and how it threads through every song he touched
• The "nine lives" connection — a private story Bon told friends that surfaces word-for-word in the title track
• Brian Johnson's work with Geordie — competent, professional, but missing the signature wit
• Why "You Shook Me All Night Long" is the smoking gun
• The question nobody asks: if Brian wrote it, why did he never write anything like it again?

This isn't about disrespecting Brian Johnson. It's about giving Bon Scott credit for what looks like his last masterpiece.
```
